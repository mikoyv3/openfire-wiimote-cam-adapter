1. Click on the PCB/PCBA block under the "Order Now" section to start your printed circuit board or assembly order. This option will take you to the file upload page where you can upload your Gerber files and configure your panelization settings.</br>
   <img width="809" height="251" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/287a8c43-66c2-4e49-a200-7227e7edb4d0" />

2. Click the blue Add gerber file button to upload your PCB design files. Upload your compressed file, named pixart-sensor-adapter-inline-connector-panel-2-vert_gerbers.zip, directly into that upload section.</br>
   <img width="745" height="372" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/63a884df-4b40-449b-983f-f89833e9355b" /></br>
   <img width="753" height="400" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cf7e225d-c6dd-4ac0-a0b1-3ce512b6ab7a" /></br>

3.  Once the upload completes, provide the panel details accordingly.</br>
   10 total PCB, set Column 1 and Row 1</br>
   20 total PCB, set Column 1 and Row 2
     <img width="745" height="347" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/73f1aab6-26c4-4b63-a8cb-61d7d9b629ab" /></br>

5.  Ensure the PCB Assembly toggled, then click the blue NEXT button to proceed.</br>
     <img width="1051" height="520" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b33fad7a-dbc0-4fd9-8367-93b523a8ce30" /></br>

6. Click the blue NEXT button on the right side of the interface to move on to the Bill of Materials (BOM) page.</br>
     <img width="1081" height="695" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1fe3ea03-86d0-4cbf-b6d5-a2aed1c34e4a" /></br>

7. Upload the BOM and CPL file, then Click the blue Process BOM & CPL button in the bottom right corner to parse your uploaded files and match your adapter components.</br>
   BOM File : pixart-sensor-adapter-inline-connector-panel-2-vert_bom.csv</br>
   CPL File : pixart-sensor-adapter-inline-connector-panel-2-vert_positions.csv</br>
